Hiring more sales reps

Each rep handles one chat at a time, works office hours and costs you a salary every month. When lots of messages arrive at once, they still get swamped… and at night nobody’s there.

Option 2 · Why it fails

WhatsApp Business on your phone

It works with a few chats. With a team, messages end up spread across several phones, nobody follows up with anyone and you never know how many sales slipped away.

“Press 1 for prices, 2 for hours.” Your customers don’t talk like that: they ask in their own words, send voice notes and leave when the bot doesn’t understand them.
